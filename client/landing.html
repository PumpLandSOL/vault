Four ways to hold the fund.
The same NAV engine, four instruments — from the simplest deposit to a one-click leverage loop.
01 · ENROLL

Dividend Program

Enroll VAULT, receive sVAULT, and compound every distribution automatically. Nothing to claim. Redemption is 1:1 and immediate — the floor is always there.

02 · BOND

Equity Bond Desk

Subscribe in USDG for VAULT notes at a discount to market, floored at NAV so every sale grows the backing. Notes vest over two days.

03 · LOOP

Loopback Credit

Borrow USDG against your enrolled shares, buy more VAULT, and enroll it — a Lombard leverage loop in a single transaction, with a live health meter.

The whole policy, in one line
"rate = R_MAX × clamp((P − 1) / (K − 1), 0, 1). Zero at NAV, full at the premium K. No admin can change it — the only way to change the dividend is to deploy a different fund."
Backed, not printed into thin air.
The difference between a reserve and a Ponzi is the floor. VAULT keeps one, on-chain, and quotes around it from both sides — buying below NAV, selling above it.
◆ FLOOR

1 USDG minimum

Every VAULT is backed by at least one USDG of risk-free reserves. NAV never counts treasury-held VAULT above that floor.

◆ PREMIUM

Earn only when it's earned

Dividends turn off at or below NAV and saturate at a 1.75× premium. The fund pays out exactly what the market's confidence justifies.

◆ ACCRETIVE

Every bond grows backing

Bonds are floored at NAV, so the treasury can never sell VAULT below its existing backing. The floor only rises.
